# 2026-03-31

## Final Obstacle Adjustment Near Goal

- Moved only `center_pole_04` outward from `y=0.95` to `y=2.10`.
- Reason: the goal point itself was clear, but the final centerline approach toward `goal_y=0.0` was too narrow near the last obstacle, causing late avoidance and `emergency_stop_obstacle`.
- Expected effect: increase clearance on the final approach and reduce last-segment emergency stops without changing the rest of the corridor layout.

## Preferred Clearance Hold Logic

- Replaced the fixed preferred clearance with `preferred_clearance_margin`.
- Planner derives its soft avoidance buffer as `emergency_stop_distance + preferred_clearance_margin`.
- Initial trial with a large margin made the vehicle too conservative in tight corridors.
- Updated the logic so the soft buffer follows the forward/target direction instead of any obstacle anywhere around the vehicle.
- Current settings: `emergency_stop_distance=1.0`, `preferred_clearance_margin=0.15`, so stronger avoidance starts at about `1.15m`.
